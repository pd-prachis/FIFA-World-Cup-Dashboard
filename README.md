📌 Problem Statement:

The FIFA World Cup is the most prestigious football tournament, rich with historical and statistical data. However, drawing actionable insights from decades of match records, team performances, and attendance trends requires structured visualization. This dashboard delivers a comprehensive, interactive analysis of World Cup history—spanning goals scored, matches played, team qualifications, and hosting patterns.

🎯 Objective:

Design an interactive Power BI dashboard that allows:

Fans, analysts, and decision-makers to explore World Cup trends

Federation stakeholders to analyze hosting impact and performance patterns

Journalists and researchers to use reliable visuals for storytelling

Users to gain a historical perspective on team and country participation

🛠️ Steps Followed:

Step 1: Loaded historical FIFA data into Power BI Desktop

Step 2: Used Power Query Editor to:

Clean inconsistencies in year, team, and country names

Standardize numeric formats for attendance, goals, and matches

Create custom columns for sorting and time-based grouping

Step 3: Created DAX measures for:

Total Goals Scored

Matches Played

Number of Qualified Teams

Attendance by Country and Year

Titles Won by Country

Step 4: Built visuals to present:

🏆 World Cup Winners by Count

🌍 Country-wise Participation & Hosting Stats

📊 Goals, Matches & Qualification by Year

🧭 Maps & bar charts to show trends across decades

Step 5: Published the dashboard to Power BI Service for real-time access and filters by year, country, and team.

🔍 Key Insights Summary:

Brazil has the most World Cup titles (5) and is the only country to qualify for every edition

European and South American nations dominate both wins and hosting responsibilities

Participation has expanded over time, making the tournament truly global

Attendance spikes in host nations like Brazil, France, and Germany highlight the event's popularity

Visual data storytelling makes it easy to explore and compare World Cup history interactively
